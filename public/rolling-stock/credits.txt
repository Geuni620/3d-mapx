Adaptation by 3d-mapx. The exterior cuboids and textures were assembled into
meter-scale GLB meshes for cab and intermediate cars. Sliding door panels
and their travel distances are retained. 3d-mapx adds time-based animation,
door indicator lamps and generic running gear. Sounds and interiors were
omitted. These assets are illustrative and are not official operator models.
